## 1. Security: patched apps may be less safe, and that's on you

Modifying an app can introduce **security vulnerabilities**, instability, crashes, data loss or
privacy problems that the original app didn't have. Patched apps are re-signed with a key that
isn't the publisher's, don't receive store updates (including security fixes), and may behave
unpredictably when a removed component is unexpectedly needed.

uncreep is designed to be driven by AI agents. AI models are good at reverse engineering and
writing code, but **different models behave differently**, make mistakes, and can misread code
or overstate findings. **uncreep has been tested against very few apps.** Every build should be
treated as experimental and unverified unless you have checked it yourself.

**The creator and contributors of this repository are not liable** for any vulnerability,
malfunction, data loss, account suspension, or other harm resulting from any app modified using
it.

## 2. Legality: make sure you're allowed before you start

Modifying, decompiling or redistributing an app **may be against the app's terms of service or
end-user licence agreement, and may be unlawful in your jurisdiction** (for example under
copyright, anti-circumvention, computer-misuse or contract law). Some jurisdictions allow
reverse engineering for interoperability, security research or personal use. Many don't, or do
only under narrow conditions.

**Only use uncreep where you are sure it is legally permitted** for you, for that app, and for
your purpose. If in doubt, take legal advice. Nothing in this repository is legal advice. "Creepy",

legal conclusions about any app or company.

Do not redistribute modified apps. They contain the original publisher's code. uncreep's
delivery tooling refuses to publish builds from public repositories for this reason.

uncreep must not be used to unlock paid features, bypass licensing, DRM or paywalls, cheat, or
undermine protections that exist for other people.
